🚨Heat Stroke Emergency Response

Heat stroke is a medical emergency. If someone exhibits confusion, body temperature > 40°C, or cessation of sweating, call emergency medical services immediately.

📞Call 108 (Ambulance - India) 📞Call 112 / 911 (All Emergencies)
Immediate First-Aid while waiting:
  • Move the patient immediately into an air-conditioned room or dense shade.
  • Apply cool wet cloths or ice packs to the neck, armpits, and groin.
  • Fan aggressively while misting with cool water.
  • Do NOT force liquids if the person is unconscious or disoriented.
